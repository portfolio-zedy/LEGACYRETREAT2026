Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a member of Living By Design Nation to attend? +
Not at all. The General Assembly is open to everyone — members and guests alike. Simply select "Guest" during registration, choose your centres, and complete your details. Members will additionally provide their Colony, Hub and Tribe.
Is the conference available virtually? +
Yes — the final Saturday (Week 4, November 28) holds virtually on Zoom, Meeting name: "Bethel", beginning at 5:00 AM WAT. Weeks 1–3 (Nov 7, 14 & 21) are in-person only at our physical centres in Abuja, Lagos and Port Harcourt.
How much does registration cost? +
Each physical centre costs ₦10,000 — one for ₦10,000, two for ₦20,000, or all three (Abuja, Lagos & Port Harcourt) for ₦30,000. Registration requires at least one physical centre; the virtual gathering on Zoom (Bethel) is a free add-on — simply tick it alongside your paid centre(s). Payments are processed securely via Paystack.
What happens after I pay? +
You will immediately receive a unique registration ID (e.g. LGC26-XXXXXX) on screen and by email. Present this ID at the entrance of any centre you selected for check-in. Your payment is verified against this same ID, so keep it safe.
Do I need to attend all four Saturdays? +
The conference runs on all Saturdays in November 2026 (Nov 7, 14, 21 & 28). While each Saturday delivers a complete experience, attending all four gives you the full benefit of the conference.
What time should I arrive or join? +
Weeks 1–3: physical centre sessions begin at 8:00 AM (WAT) — please arrive by 7:30 AM for a smooth check-in. Week 4 (Nov 28): the virtual gathering on Zoom (Bethel) begins at 5:00 AM (WAT) — kindly join a few minutes early with your registration ID.
